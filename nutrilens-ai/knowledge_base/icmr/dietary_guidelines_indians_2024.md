# ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians (2024 Edition)

## 1. Indian Context & Macronutrient Targets
- Published by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) and National Institute of Nutrition (NIN), Hyderabad.
- **Recommended Daily Allowance (RDA)**:
  - Energy: ~2110 kcal/day (sedentary male), ~1660 kcal/day (sedentary female).
  - Protein: 0.83 g/kg body weight per day (approx. 45-55g/day for average adult).
  - Carbohydrates: 45% to 55% of total calorie intake (emphasizing complex carbohydrates, whole grains, pulses, legumes).
  - Added Sugars: Maximum 25g/day (or 5% of energy). Sugar-sweetened beverages (SSBs) and commercial fruit juices should be minimized.
  - Visible Oil/Fat: 20-30g per day per person, balanced between MUFA, PUFA, and low SFA.

## 2. Guidelines for Metabolic Health & Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)
- **Diabetes Mellitus (Type 2)**:
  - Avoid refined carbohydrate sources: Maida (refined wheat flour), polished rice, corn starch, maltodextrin.
  - High dietary fiber (>30g/day) from whole grains, pulses, vegetables slows down glucose absorption and improves insulin sensitivity.
  - Watch for hidden sugars in packaged health drinks, cereal bars, ketchup, and flavored yogurts (maltose, liquid glucose, high fructose corn syrup, invert sugar syrup).
- **Hypertension & Heart Health**:
  - Limit salt to <5g/day. Restrict sodium-dense additives: Monosodium glutamate (MSG / INS 621), Baking powder (Sodium bicarbonate), Sodium alginate, Sodium benzoate.
  - Increase potassium-rich natural foods (banana, leafy greens, coconut water) to counter sodium toxicity, provided kidney function is normal.
- **Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)**:
  - Restrict dietary sodium (<1500 mg/day).
  - Restrict inorganic phosphorus additives (Phosphoric acid INS 338, Sodium polyphosphates INS 452) found in colas, processed cheeses, packaged bakery goods. Inorganic phosphorus is absorbed at near 100% efficiency, promoting vascular calcification and mineral bone disorder in CKD.
- **Pregnancy & Lactation**:
  - Increased requirement of Iron (27 mg/day), Folate (570 mcg/day), and Calcium (1000 mg/day).
  - Strict avoidance of synthetic colors (INS 102 Tartrazine, INS 110 Sunset Yellow), artificial preservatives, high caffeine (>200mg/day), and non-nutritive artificial sweeteners.

## 3. Front-of-Pack Labelling & High-Risk Additive Alerts
- ICMR guidelines strongly recommend reading food labels for:
  1. Ingredient order (ingredients are listed in descending order by weight; if sugar or oil is in top 3, product is unhealthy).
  2. Trans Fat content ("0g trans fat" claim requires <0.2g per serving, but hydrogenated vegetable oils in ingredient list indicate hidden trans fat).
  3. Maltodextrin and Liquid Glucose: High Glycemic Index carbohydrate additives that spike blood sugar faster than table sugar.
