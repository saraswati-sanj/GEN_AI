# FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) Regulations & Guidelines

## 1. High Fat, Sugar, and Salt (HFSS) Thresholds
Under the Food Safety and Standards (Packaging and Labelling) Regulations and proposed Front-of-Pack Labelling (FOPL) guidelines, packaged food products are categorized as HFSS if nutrients exceed the following limits per 100g or 100ml:
- **Added Sugars**: Exceeding 10g per 100g (solid food) or 5g per 100ml (beverage) is considered High Sugar. Recommended daily limit of added sugar for adults is 25g (5% of total calorie intake).
- **Sodium / Salt**: Sodium exceeding 350mg per 100g (approx. 0.87g salt per 100g) in snacks, noodles, or processed foods is flagged as High Sodium. Total daily recommended sodium limit is 2,000mg (5g salt).
- **Saturated Fats**: Saturated fat exceeding 5g per 100g in solid food or 2.5g per 100ml in liquid food is High Saturated Fat.
- **Trans Fats**: FSSAI strictly limits industrial trans fats to maximum **2% by weight** of total oil/fat in all food products. Partially hydrogenated oils (PHO) are banned.

## 2. Common INS Food Additive Codes & FSSAI Safety Standards
- **INS 621 (Monosodium Glutamate / MSG)**: Permitted flavour enhancer. Must be declared on package. Contraindicated for infant foods. Excessive intake may trigger headache, flushing, or Chinese Restaurant Syndrome in sensitive individuals.
- **INS 102 (Tartrazine / Yellow 5)**: Synthetic azo dye. Permitted up to 100 mg/kg in specified foods. Can trigger hyperactivity in children and allergic reactions (hives, asthma) in aspirin-sensitive people.
- **INS 110 (Sunset Yellow FCF)**: Permitted food color. Restricted limit 100 ppm. Linked to allergy risks and child behavioral issues.
- **INS 211 (Sodium Benzoate)**: Chemical preservative in acidic foods and beverages. Allowed up to 120-600 ppm. When combined with Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid), can form trace Benzene, a known carcinogen.
- **INS 220 (Sulfur Dioxide)**: Preservative and antioxidant. Sulfites can cause severe bronchospasm in asthmatic patients. Must be declared if exceeding 10 mg/kg.
- **INS 320 (BHA - Butylated Hydroxyanisole)** & **INS 321 (BHT - Butylated Hydroxytoluene)**: Synthetic antioxidants used in oils and snacks. FSSAI limit max 200 ppm. Classified as potential endocrine disruptors and possible carcinogens in high doses.
- **INS 471 (Mono- and Diglycerides of Fatty Acids)**: Emulsifier used in bakery, ice cream, and peanut butter. Generally Safe (GRAS), but may contain hidden trans fats and contributes to lipid load.
- **INS 950 (Acesulfame Potassium)** & **INS 951 (Aspartame)**: Artificial non-nutritive sweeteners. Must carry mandatory warnings: "Not recommended for children", "Contains Phenylalanine" (for Aspartame - Phenylketonuria risk).
- **INS 955 (Sucralose)**: High-intensity artificial sweetener. Heat-stable up to moderate cooking, but degradation at high temperature may release chloropropanols.

## 3. Mandatory Allergen Declarations (FSSAI 2020 Rules)
FSSAI mandates explicit declaration of top allergens if present as ingredients or potential cross-contamination traces:
1. Cereals containing gluten (wheat, rye, barley, oats).
2. Crustaceans and products thereof.
3. Milk and milk products (including lactose).
4. Eggs and egg products.
5. Fish and fish products.
6. Peanuts, tree nuts, and nut products.
7. Soybeans and soybean products.
8. Sulfites in concentrations of 10 mg/kg or more.

## 4. Special Health Profile Warnings
- **Diabetic Individuals**: Foods with high Glycemic Index (GI), added sugars (>5g/serving), or high refined flour (maida) content trigger acute postprandial glucose spikes.
- **Hypertensive Individuals**: Products containing >350mg sodium/100g, hidden sodium sources (sodium benzoate, MSG, baking soda/sodium bicarbonate), or high salt exacerbate blood pressure elevation.
- **Kidney Disease (CKD)**: High potassium, high phosphorus additives (dicalcium phosphate, polyphosphates INS 450/452), and excessive protein load require strict monitoring.
- **Pregnancy**: High artificial sweetener intake, synthetic azo dyes (INS 102, INS 110), excess sodium, and unpasteurized ingredients are restricted.
